This Privacy Policy explains how TagLauncher handles information.

## Summary

TagLauncher is designed as a local macOS utility. It does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or telemetry. TagLauncher does not sell personal information and does not track users across apps or websites.

## Information TagLauncher Handles Locally

TagLauncher may access or store the following information on your Mac:

- A local list of installed applications, used to build the app launcher grid and search index.
- App names, bundle identifiers, icons, tags, notes, category layout, display preferences, and keyboard shortcut settings.
- Imported or exported category/layout files that you explicitly choose through macOS file panels.

This information is used only to provide TagLauncher's app organization, launch, search, import/export, and settings features.

## Data Collection

TagLauncher does not collect personal data from you or transmit personal data to the developer.

TagLauncher does not use:

- Analytics services.
- Advertising networks.
- Third-party tracking SDKs.
- Crash reporting SDKs.
- Remote telemetry.
- User behavior profiling.

## Network Access

TagLauncher does not require a network service to organize or launch your local apps.

If you open external links such as Help, Support, Privacy Policy, or release links, macOS may open those links in your web browser. Those external websites are subject to their own privacy practices.

## Local Storage

TagLauncher stores preferences and app organization data locally on your Mac, using standard macOS storage such as UserDefaults and the user's Application Support folder.

You can remove local TagLauncher data by deleting the app and its related local support files, subject to normal macOS behavior.

## User-Selected Files

TagLauncher may read from or write to files only when you choose import or export locations through macOS file panels. TagLauncher uses those files only for the import/export operation you requested.
